About Me

I grew up in a small town in Nashik district with an early curiosity about why people think and feel the way they do. That curiosity led me to psychology — not just as a subject, but as a lens through which I began to understand human connection, resilience, and growth.

My academic journey took me from Nashik to Pune, where I completed my Master's in Clinical Psychology at Abasaheb Garware College. Along the way, I worked in counselling centres, rehabilitation facilities, and special schools — each experience teaching me something textbooks never could. I was also part of the National Mental Health Survey (NMHS-2) team, collecting field data across Maharashtra and learning firsthand how mental health intersects with communities.

Today, I'm pursuing my B.Ed because I believe education and mental health are inseparable. Every student I meet reminds me why I chose this path — and why I keep going.

"Psychology is not just my profession — it is how I understand the world."
